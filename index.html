About

I study how three-dimensional (3D) genome organization and epigenomic regulation shape gene expression and cell fate during mammalian development. I received my MD and PhD from Kyoto University, where I investigated mechanisms of genome reorganization during mammalian germline development in the laboratory of Mitinori Saitou. I am currently an HFSP Long-Term Fellow in Anders Hansen’s laboratory at MIT, where I combine ultra-high-resolution 3D genomics, epigenomics, genetic perturbations, and quantitative modeling to uncover regulatory principles governing mammalian germ-cell differentiation.
